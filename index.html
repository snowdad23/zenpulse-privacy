Snowdev Studios · ZenPulse

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Snowdev Studios develops ZenPulse, a breathing and meditation app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and how you can delete it. Contact us at prateekbehera1402@gmail.com.

Information saved on your device

ZenPulse saves completed session types, session identifiers, durations and completion dates, total mindful minutes, streaks, and settings such as haptics, cue sounds, ambient audio and cycle count. These records support your history, progress and preferences. They are stored in the app's local storage; Snowdev Studios does not receive local-only records.

Your operating system may include local app data in device backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. Those backups are controlled by your device or backup provider. Deleting data inside ZenPulse does not directly remove a previously created device backup.

Cloud services and online audio

ZenPulse can run without cloud services. Settings → Privacy & data shows whether cloud services are enabled in your installed app. When enabled, Google Firebase Authentication assigns an account identifier, and Cloud Firestore stores your session history and progress under that identifier. The current account system can use a generated anonymous account; it does not require you to enter a name or email address.

When these online services are used, Google and the media host receive the network information needed to deliver and secure their services, such as your IP address and request information. Online audio requests are sent to the configured media host, which may be Firebase Storage. We use this processing to provide authentication, progress storage and audio playback, not advertising. Remote audio is requested over HTTPS.

The app does not currently include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, precise location or Health Connect data, and it does not record your breathing or audio. Practice history describes app sessions, not measurements of your body.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information or use your practice history for advertising. When cloud features are active, information described above is processed by Google Firebase to provide those features. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information if required by a valid legal obligation.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, medical records or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Cloud records, when cloud services are enabled, remain associated with the account until deleted. Uninstalling alone does not delete a cloud account.

Open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my data and account. This ends active sessions, clears local history and preferences, and, when connected to cloud services, deletes the current account's history, profile and authentication account. Cloud deletion requires an internet connection. If deletion fails, the app reports the failure so you can retry. A later app session may create a new anonymous account when cloud services are enabled.

You can also request deletion outside the app. We may need to verify ownership before deleting cloud data. We cannot remotely erase local-only data from your device. Service-provider operational logs and device backups are subject to the provider's retention and deletion processes; the in-app action does not promise immediate deletion of those separate copies. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

Security and your choices

We restrict account data access to the authenticated owner and use HTTPS for online media. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Local preferences and history are not protected by a separate app password; protect your device and backups. You can use available local breathing features without cloud connectivity, adjust session settings, and delete your data at any time.

Children and wellbeing

ZenPulse is a general wellbeing tool and does not diagnose, treat or monitor medical conditions. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when the app's data practices change. Contact Snowdev Studios about privacy, access or deletion at prateekbehera1402@gmail.com.
