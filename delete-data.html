Delete your account and data

You can delete your ZenPulse data from the app or contact Snowdev Studios without reinstalling the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open ZenPulse → Settings → Privacy & data.
  2. Select “Delete my data and account” and confirm.
  3. Stay connected to the internet if cloud services are enabled. If the app reports a failure, retry.

This removes local session history, mindful minutes, streaks and preferences. If a cloud account exists, including when sync has been switched off, it also removes the current account's cloud history, profile and authentication account. Uninstalling the app alone does not remove cloud records.

Request deletion by email

Email prateekbehera1402@gmail.com with the subject “ZenPulse data deletion request”. Tell us whether you want your account and all associated app data deleted, or only specified cloud session records. If you still have access to the app and cloud services are enabled, include the account ID displayed in Privacy & data to help identify your records. Do not send passwords or medical information.

We will reply to help identify the account and verify that you are authorized to request deletion. An anonymous account is not linked to your email address, so an email address alone may not identify it. The in-app deletion option is the most direct way to verify access to the current account.

Local data and retained copies

We cannot erase local-only records remotely. Use the in-app deletion action, Android's clear-storage option, or uninstall the app to remove those records from your device. Manage device backups separately through your backup provider. In-app deletion does not directly remove earlier backups or service-provider operational logs, which follow the provider's retention processes. We keep deletion-request correspondence only as needed to resolve it and meet applicable obligations.
